Field Guide

Emergence & Physics Explanations

What is Particle Life?

Particle Life is a continuous multi-agent artificial life (A-Life) simulation first popularized by researchers like Jeffrey Ventrella. Unlike cellular automata which operate on a rigid discrete grid, particles here move freely in continuous space under simple Newtonian-like attraction and repulsion forces.

✦ How Emergence Happens

There are no hardcoded instructions telling particles to create "cells", "hunters", or "rotating spirals". Instead, complex macroscopic structures spontaneously self-organize from microscopic local interactions. Simple local rules compound into life-like behavior.

Breaking Newton's Third Law

In standard physics, every action has an equal and opposite reaction (FAB = -FBA). In Particle Life, forces are intentionally asymmetric: Species A can be strongly attracted to Species B, while Species B is fiercely repelled by Species A.

This creates an eternal pursuit: Species A chases Species B across the canvas, while Species B flees, generating continuous kinetic propulsion and perpetual organic movement without external energy injection.

The Physics & Mathematics

For any two particles separated by distance d, the net force F(d) is determined by two critical distance thresholds and the interaction matrix Ai,j:

0 < d < rmin F(d) = -1.2 × (1 - d / rmin) Core Repulsion (prevents collapse)
rmin ≤ d ≤ rmax F(d) = Ai,j × (1 - |2d - (rmax + rmin)| / (rmax - rmin)) Inter-Species Force Ramp
d > rmax F(d) = 0 Out of interaction range

Key Parameters:

  • rmin (Core Repulsion Radius): Short-range repulsive force acting like an atomic Pauli exclusion principle. It prevents particles from condensing into an infinitely dense point.
  • rmax (Maximum Influence Radius): The perceptual boundary. Particles outside this radius exert zero force on each other.
  • Viscosity / Friction (μ): Applied to velocity every frame: vt+1 = (vt + a · Δt) × μ. Damps kinetic chaos and allows stable equilibrium structures to crystallize.
  • Spatial Hash Partitioning: The simulation space is divided into bins of size rmax. Particles only query their 9 immediate neighboring buckets, turning an otherwise sluggish O(N2) algorithm into blazing O(N) running 60 FPS for thousands of particles.

Curated Biomes Field Guide

🧬 Cellular Mitosis

Species form outer protective membrane shells with high mutual surface tension that enclose inner nucleus cores. When perturbations occur, the outer membrane stretches and pinches, mimicking biological cell division (mitosis).

⚔️ Predator & Prey

An asymmetric food chain cycle: Ruby fiercely pursues Teal, Teal evades Ruby while hunting Amber, and Amber circles back around. The population dynamic produces pulsating migratory waves and pack hunting.

🌌 Spiral Disk (Galaxies)

Configured with angular force asymmetries that convert radial attraction into orbital angular momentum. Forms rotating galactic disks with distinct spiraling arms and a super-dense core.

🐍 Serpent Chains

A specialized non-commutative matrix where each species is exclusively attracted to the subsequent species in a ring (1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 1). Forms long, flexible, self-steering serpentine filaments.

❄️ Crystal Net

Symmetric matrices with strong positive self-attraction and balanced inter-species repulsion produce static, perfectly spaced crystallographic lattices and molecular lattices.
